English Summary: The vagina naturally produces odorless, watery discharge, which varies  
with ovulation, pregnancy, and medications. The low (acidic) pH creates an inhospitable  
environment for pathogens. Colors indicate causes: Clear (Normal/Ovulation), White/Clumpy  
(Yeast), Yellow/Green (STI), Grey (Bacterial Vaginosis).

~20% of patients, is odorless, and resembles cottage cheese. Triggers include antibiotics,  
corticosteroids, oral contraceptives, poorly controlled diabetes, pregnancy, and douching.
