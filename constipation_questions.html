D) 80%

mnemonic, what does the 'I' stand for?  
A) Ibuprofen  
B) Insulin

side eﬀect?  
A) SSRIs  
B) Tricyclic antidepressants (TCAs)  
C) MAOIs  
D) SNRIs

7. Opioids cause constipation primarily by which mechanism?  
A) Increasing gastric acid  
B) Decreasing gut motility and ﬂuid secretion  
C) Destroying gut bacteria  
D) Increasing heart rate  
 Hint: They bind to receptors in the GI tract, eﬀectively paralyzing the bowel's normal rhythmic  
contractions.

possible?  
A) They cause fetal blindness  
B) They cross the placenta and sedate the baby  
C) They can cause uterine muscle contractions  
D) They taste bad to pregnant women

A) Water  
B) Antacids or milk  
C) Bread

A) Stimulants  
B) Bulk-forming  
C) Osmotic laxatives  
D) Prokinetics

A) 30 minutes  
B) 6 hours  
C) 48 hours or longer  
D) 1 week  
 Hint: It takes a couple of days to draw enough ﬂuid into the bowel to be eﬀective.
