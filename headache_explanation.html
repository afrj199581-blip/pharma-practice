English Summary: Headaches are classified by the IHS into 3 types: 1. Primary  
Headaches: Caused by the condition itself without underlying structural pathology (Migraine,  
Tension). 2. Secondary Headaches: Attributable to another condition (Infection, Stroke). 3.  
Cranial Neuralgias: Nerve-related head pain.

English Summary: Primary Headaches: 1. Tension-Type (TTH): Bilateral, band-like,  
mild-moderate pressure. 2. Migraine: Unilateral, throbbing pain with nausea/photo-phobia,  
aggravated by activity. 3. Cluster: Severe, piercing unilateral (periorbital) pain with autonomic  
symptoms (lacrimation, rhinorrhea).
