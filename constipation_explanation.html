reduction in normal bowel habits (<3 per week), difficult defecation, or hard stools. It is  
highly prevalent, affecting 25-40% of people >65 years, and is 2-3x more common in  
women (40% incidence in late pregnancy).

English Summary: Causes include poor diet (low fluid/fiber), lack of exercise, and ignoring  
the urge to defecate. Many drugs cause constipation (mnemonic CONSTIPation: Calcium,  
Opioids, Non-DHP CCBs, Sucralfate, TCAs, Iron, Phenothiazines). Serious organic causes  
like colorectal cancer and hypothyroidism must be ruled out.
