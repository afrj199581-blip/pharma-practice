Rhinitis is the inflammation and swelling of the mucous membrane inside the nose. It is  
considered a chronic inflammatory upper airway disease and a major cause for pharmacy  
consultations due to its impact on sleep and productivity.

Rhinitis is classified into 4 types: 1. Allergic (AR) - IgE-mediated response. 2. Non-Allergic  
(NAR) - Nasal reactivity to environment without histamine. 3. Rhinitis Medicamentosa -  
Rebound congestion from overuse of decongestants. 4. Infective Rhinitis - Viral/bacterial  
origin.
