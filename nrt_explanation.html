nicotine without the harmful toxins (tar, carbon monoxide) found in cigarettes. This reduces  
withdrawal symptoms and cravings, making quitting manageable and breaking the addiction  
gradually.

levels for 16-24h). 2. Short-Acting (Gums, Lozenges, Inhalers, Nasal Sprays - used for rapid  
relief of sudden cravings).  
:(Clinical Pearl)

Gum (GIT issues from vigorous chewing; avoid in denture wearers). Lozenges (Mouth  
irritation/heartburn). Nasal Spray (Rhinitis - don't sniff/swallow while spraying).  
:(Clinical Pearl)
