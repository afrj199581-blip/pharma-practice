A) Streptococcus  
B) Staphylococcus

A) Lacrimal gland  
B) Sweat gland  
C) Meibomian gland  
D) Sebaceous cyst  
 Hint: These glands produce the oily layer of the tear ﬁlm and are located along the rim of the  
eyelids.  
3. Which of the following chronic skin conditions can frequently inﬂame the eyelids?  
A) Psoriasis and Eczema  
B) Seborrhoeic dermatitis and Rosacea  
C) Vitiligo  
D) Melanoma  
 Hint: One causes dandruﬀ-like ﬂakes, and the other causes facial redness and visible blood  
vessels.

A) They cause allergic rhinitis

C) They block the lacrimal punctum

infection?  
A) Using daily disposables  
B) Cleaning with sterile saline  
C) Overnight wear and poor handling  
D) Wearing them for less than 4 hours  
 Hint: Sleeping with foreign bodies in the eye traps bacteria and reduces oxygen to the cornea.  
7. Which systemic condition makes a patient more susceptible to severe eyelid  
infections?  
A) Hypertension  
B) Asthma  
C) Diabetes  
D) Osteoporosis  
 Hint: This condition causes poor wound healing and immunosuppression, often associated with  
high blood sugar.

12. What visual observation would strongly suggest the presence of blepharitis or  
Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD)?  
A) Pale conjunctiva  
B) Red lid margins, inspissated meibum, and lash crusting  
C) Completely clear sclera  
D) Drooping of the upper eyelid  
 Hint: Look for signs of inﬂammation and blockage right where the eyelashes grow.  
13. What is a core component of daily eyelid hygiene for self-care?  
A) Washing with harsh antibacterial soap  
B) Cleaning the lids with diluted baby shampoo  
C) Scrubbing the cornea with a cotton swab  
D) Using alcohol wipes on the eyelashes  
 Hint: The product used should be gentle and 'tear-free' to avoid stinging the eye.  
14. How long should warm compresses be applied to be eﬀective?  
A) 1-2 minutes  
B) 10-15 minutes  
C) 30-45 minutes  
D) 60 minutes  
 Hint: It needs enough time to melt the thickened oils in the glands, but not so long as to burn the  
skin.  
15. What is the recommended frequency for applying warm compresses daily?  
A) Once a week  
B) 2 to 4 times a day  
C) Only when pain is severe  
D) Every hour  
 Hint: Consistency throughout the day is key to keeping the glands open.

 Hint: You need a medication that directly kills the causative bacteria.

B) Moderate to severe infection or preseptal cellulitis  
C) Occasional dry eye

A) Sjogren's syndrome  
B) Natural aging  
C) Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD) and environmental factors  
D) Use of contact lenses solely

following?  
A) Working in a dusty environment  
B) Aging and autoimmune conditions like Sjogren's syndrome  
C) Wearing old mascara

26. Reﬂex or neuropathic dry eye is primarily triggered by what?  
A) Overhydration  
B) Corneal nerve irritation or ocular surface disease  
C) Excessive blinking  
D) High humidity

produce tears.  
27. Which environmental factor strongly exacerbates dry eye symptoms?  
A) High humidity  
B) Smoke, dry air, and wind  
C) Wearing sunglasses  
D) Using a humidiﬁer  
 Hint: These conditions increase the rate at which tears turn into vapor.

D) Tell them to pop the lump
